What Business ADRs Should Capture
The market and competitive positioning decision
Why this disease area? Why now? What does the competitive landscape look like and what assumptions does the platform bet on? This is the ADR everything else implicitly depends on, but it's usually never written down.

Build vs. buy vs. partner decisions
For each major capability (data acquisition, analytics, regulatory submission tooling), what was considered and why was it rejected? These decisions get revisited constantly as the company scales and people forget why the original choice was made.

The data monetization or sharing strategy
Will data products ever be shared externally — with CROs, academic partners, regulators, or commercial partners? This has massive downstream consequences for the governance architecture you've already designed and should be an explicit decision, not an assumption.

Organizational scaling assumptions
The Hub/Spoke model in ADR-3 assumes domains will eventually own their data products. When does that transition happen? What triggers it — headcount, funding round, regulatory milestone? Without this, the Hub team expands indefinitely and the mesh never matures.
